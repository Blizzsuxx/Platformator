## Actual source owners and consumers

- Body registration, active integration, movement scratch and solver scratch: [PhysicsManager](../../../src/physicsmanager.cpp), with membership/changed-body and sleep union-find in [body indexes](../../../src/physicsbodyindexes.cpp). Declarations are in [Rigidbody](../../../include/platformator/rigidbody.h) and [PhysicsManager](../../../src/physicsmanager.h).
- Net support ledger/queue, all-contact and solver memberships and unsorted solver membership: [contact maintenance](../../../src/physicscontacts.cpp), [pair state](../../../src/colliderpair.h) and [Collision support computation](../../../src/collision.cpp). Contact geometry owns only the pair's current Collision, manifold and version stamps.
- Pair slots, adjacency, witness counts, pending pair/collider/cell queues and reductions: [Grid](../../../src/grid.cpp), [Grid declarations](../../../src/grid.h), and [Collider synchronization](../../../src/collider.cpp). Segmented interval/checkpoint algorithms and cell buffers remain outside the changed implementation.

These queues have different validity boundaries and cannot be treated as duplicates.

## Baseline reduction targets

J4/J5: supportBodyA, supportBodyB, supportsA, supportsB are four independently written pair fields. reducePairSupport compares and writes body and support flag together for each endpoint; removePair tests flags and reads corresponding old bodies before resetting flags. Planned: two nullable credited-recipient identities, used by the same reconcile/release operation. No independent cached current-body snapshot is needed when no contribution exists.

J6: isQueuedForNarrowPhase and narrowPhaseQueueIndex are two independent membership fields. Grid enqueue and dequeue and worker consumption each write both. Planned: one index; the read-only query derives from its sentinel. Pending batch storage still exists during consumed-worker processing; it is not another membership flag.

Do not mutate key identity in the unordered pair set. No new cached body/contact eligibility or replacement flag is permitted. Retained counts, queues, islands and indexes must keep the distinct consumers above.

## US1 actual disposition

Body families are retained with explicit names: registeredBodies, awakeDynamicBodies/awakeDynamicIndex, movingKinematicBodies/movingKinematicIndex, sleepCandidates/sleepCandidateIndex and pendingBodyChanges/participationQueueIndex. queueBodyParticipationChange, cancelBodyParticipationChange, reconcileChangedBodies and reconcileBodyParticipation clarify pending versus derived work. Geometry entry is physics_geometry::updateContactGeometry(pair, gravityDirection); shape/clipping helpers and fallback math are private implementation functions. Only unreachable circle branches in the box-only clipping helper were deleted. Collision's private fallback method is removed after its unchanged implementation moved; no public operation or object layout field changes there. Other inventory rows are retained as recorded, including body/delta queues, all-contact/solver oracle and witness/adjacency/scratch/cache families. Audit-after.md provides all eight actual owner/consumer routes.

Final reset refinement: resetSleepIsland has one definition in src/physicsmanager.h, permitting Release inlining from the force worker and body reconciliation. Its four scratch fields remain unchanged. No extra body state, dirty flag or duplicate definition replaces the removed cross-file call. All inventory families have a recorded retained/renamed/removed disposition; the only owned fact reductions remain the two support flags and narrow-phase queue flag.

Current solver-order disposition (2026-10-26): `orderSolverPairs` and its comparator/sort/reindex helper/call plus `solverOrderDirty` are removed.
