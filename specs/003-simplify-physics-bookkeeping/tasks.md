## Format: `[ID] [P?] [Story?] Description`

- Every task has a completion box, sequential ID and concrete file/output paths.
- `[P]` means the task may run alongside its explicitly listed peer after their common prerequisite, with distinct file ownership. It does not authorize extra agents by itself or override measurement/resource isolation.
- Story-phase tasks have `[US1]`, `[US2]` or `[US3]`; setup, foundation and polish tasks have no story label.
- Evidence paths are outputs to create, not proof already collected. Large raw captures may live in a fresh ignored `bin/physics-bookkeeping.*` directory with exact location, source snapshots, manifests and checksums recorded in feature evidence.
