# US2: sleeping and waking audit

Before source is retained under bin/runtime-cleanup-validation/baseline-source-v2. Final starting points: [Rigidbody](../../src/rigidbody.cpp), [body participation and roots](../../src/physicsbodyindexes.cpp), [physics stages](../../src/physicsmanager.cpp), [inline reset](../../src/physicsmanager.h).

- **J4 entering sleep**: Each awake body resets its own step-local node during independent force work. Contact solver preparation unions eligible candidates. `updateSleepingStates` first reduces every member's quiet motion and minimum persistent timer into its root, then makes decisions in a separate traversal. Participation drains after both traversals, so sleeping cannot invalidate the iterated list. `findSleepRoot` now names the action; root discovery, path compression and union rank remain explicit. No per-step island objects or global scans replace the intrusive scratch.
- **J5 waking**: Explicit/setter/transform paths lead to `setIsSleeping(false)`. Its unchanged-state guard preserves accumulated quiet time. A real transition queues participation and resets the timer once; only sleep entry clears motion and force. Contact impact uses `hasWakeMotion(other)` with the same thresholds, including tiny moving kinematics; the unused receiver argument is gone. Post-solver reconciliation admits late-woken candidates and resets their scratch before the sleep pass, then movement resumes from the resulting list.
- **Support and lifetime**: Pair contributor pointers identify old/new body recipients. Net body deltas commit before deciding final support loss, so transfer does not wake at an intermediate zero. Old allocations survive the flush and callback drain. This mechanism was retained without rewriting contacts.

Concrete reductions: duplicate transition timer assignments and obsolete branch logging are removed; the impact predicate has one actual input; stale commented reconciliation is gone. Braces and stage spacing expose the remaining sequence. Evidence: [characterization](evidence/us2-characterization.md), [results](evidence/us2-results.md). Maintainer review of whether these routes read clearly remains pending.
