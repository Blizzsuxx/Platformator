# Final engine controls

The final matched Release capture contains 32 sweep jobs: four scenarios (broad-phase lanes, dense broad-phase, moving add/remove with 1,000 steady objects, and persistent contacts), two populations, workers 1 and 6, and baseline/candidate. Each timing cell uses five independent processes, 20 warmup and 60 measured steps, seed 0 and affinity 0,2,4,6,8,10; the shuffled schedule is under `engine-benchmarks/`, with manifests, raw samples and logs in `engine-benchmarks/raw.tar.xzdata`. This shorter capture provides less tail confidence than the repository's 120/600-step example. Validation runs are separate from timing. All 16 baseline/candidate matching physical-invariant manifest objects were exactly equal for the same scenario, population and worker count. Arbitrary unconstrained solver trajectories were not certified.

`engine-benchmarks/comparison.csv` records complete-step means, mean per-process p95s and between-process standard deviations. In the first final capture, one-worker dense/50 was 0.1593 ms candidate versus 0.1401 ms baseline (candidate run standard deviation 0.0260 ms); persistent/100 was 0.00765 versus 0.00648 ms (candidate deviation 0.00171 ms). A separate five-process-per-version repeat (`engine-followup-final/`) gave dense/50 0.1390 versus 0.1377 ms and persistent/100 0.00747 versus 0.00758 ms; the initial differences did not reproduce. The relevant nested physics, broad-phase and cleanup means/p95s are in `engine-benchmarks/nested-comparison.csv`; nested scopes are not additive to complete-step. Additional workloads, longer tail capture and graphical rendering remain unverified.

Pre-review and potentially contaminated earlier captures are retained under their explicitly labelled directories and are not used as final-source evidence. An initially attempted follow-up with only one repetition was rejected by the sweep tool; the failed logs remain in `engine-followup/`.
